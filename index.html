How to play

  • Click a cell to open it. Right-click (or Flag mode) to place a flag.
  • A number tells how many mines touch that cell. Chord: click an open number once its flags match to open the rest.
  • Your first click is always safe. On mobile, use Flag mode to place flags.

Fully offline. No account, no network calls, no data leaves your device. Best times are stored only in your browser. A game of logic — flags mark your guesses, they are never checked against the board until you open a cell.
